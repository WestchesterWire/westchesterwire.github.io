🏛 Politics & Government

They want to restart Indian Point. Westchester says no.

Westchester County Government  ·  March 2026

It's back. The Indian Point nuclear power plant — which sat on the Hudson River in Buchanan for decades before its final reactor was shut down in 2021 — is being floated again in federal energy circles as a potential solution to the country's growing power demands. And for many Westchester residents, that's not a comfortable conversation.

County Executive Ken Jenkins has released a statement making the county's position clear: he opposes restarting the plant. This isn't just politics — Indian Point sits less than 35 miles from midtown Manhattan, in a county of nearly a million people. The decommissioning process is still underway. The idea of reversing course raises real questions about safety, community input, and who gets to make that call.

"This is not an abstract energy policy debate. This is our community." — County Executive Ken Jenkins

For now, nothing has been decided at the federal level. But if this is a conversation you care about, this is a good moment to pay attention — and to let your representatives know where you stand.
 
💼 Business & Economy

Neiman Marcus is leaving The Westchester — and taking the county's last luxury anchor with it

The Neiman Marcus at The Westchester mall in White Plains will close as part of the Saks Global bankruptcy restructuring, leaving Westchester County without a major luxury retailer and raising real questions about what fills that footprint in one of the county's most prominent shopping destinations.

No closing date has been set yet. In the meantime — maybe one last trip to the shoe department is warranted.
 
🚨 Public Safety

Rabid coyote confirmed near Eastchester and Bronxville — keep pets inside

Westchester County has confirmed a rabid coyote in the Eastchester/Bronxville area, and a second suspected case has been reported nearby. This is the real deal — health officials are asking residents to keep pets inside, avoid contact with any wildlife acting strangely, and report sightings immediately.

Coyotes have been increasingly common in Westchester suburbs over the past few years. This is a good reminder that they're wild animals. If you or a pet are bitten or scratched, contact the Westchester County Department of Health at (914) 813-5000 right away.
 
🎉 Community

Tarrytown and Sleepy Hollow brought the energy for St. Patrick's Day

Thousands of residents lined Main Street in Tarrytown and Route 9 through Sleepy Hollow on Sunday, March 15 for the annual St. Patrick's Day parade. Engines, sirens, drums, horns — the whole production. The Rivertowns showed out.

These are the moments that remind you why living here is special. If you missed it — there's always next year. And honestly, start planning now, because the crowd only grows.
